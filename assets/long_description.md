**Orchard** connects Claude to the apps you live in on your Mac: Reminders, Calendar, Notes, Mail, Messages and Contacts.

- **Reminders & Calendar** through a native EventKit helper: multiple alerts ("two days before"), repeats, priorities, lists, batch creation, complete and delete.
- **Mail**: instant search across every mailbox with previews, full message reading, drafts and replies that open for your review, flag, move and archive.
- **Messages**: unread conversations, history by person, search, and sending to people or group chats after you confirm.
- **Notes**: Markdown in and out, search, folders, append, move.
- **Today**: one call to see your day across all of them.

Everything runs locally on your Mac. The native helper is compiled on first launch (needs Apple's Command Line Tools) and asks macOS for each permission under its own name, "Orchard Helper".
